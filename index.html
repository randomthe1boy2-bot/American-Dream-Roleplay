Community Standards

Rule Book

Please review our rules carefully before applying or joining our Discord.

====================================|

This is a server with Freedom of speech.

====================================|

We encourage organic roleplay and unscripted character interactions while ensuring hate speech and toxic harassment are strictly prohibited.

1

Keep everyone feeling welcome!

Treat fellow community members with basic kindness and hospitality. We aim to keep American Dream RP an inviting space for all players.

2

No racism / transphobia / Homophobia

Discrimination or hate speech targeting race, gender identity, or sexual orientation will lead to an immediate permanent ban without warning.

3

18+ only

All community members must be at least 18 years of age to ensure a mature roleplaying environment across both Discord and FiveM.

4

Follow discord TOS

Adhere strictly to Discord's official Terms of Service and Community Guidelines in all text and voice channels.
